# Code of conduct

Patchboard follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep criticism on the code. No
harassment, insults or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or
anywhere else you speak for the project.

To report a problem, email github@nbnstack.com. Reports stay private. The
maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who
breaks these rules.
